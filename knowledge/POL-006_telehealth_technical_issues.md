If a video visit fails to connect, resend the visit link and ask the patient to refresh the page or try a different browser.

If the visit still cannot start after 10 minutes, offer to move to a phone call if the provider agrees, or reschedule.

When the problem was on the clinic side, the rescheduled visit has no fee. Report platform failures to operations the same day.
