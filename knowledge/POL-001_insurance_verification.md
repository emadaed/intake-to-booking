---
id: POL-001
title: Insurance verification before the first visit
status: current
effective_date: 2026-06-01
owner: Intake team
---
Verify insurance benefits within 2 business days of a booking and before the first visit.

Check the plan type, whether telehealth nutrition counseling is covered, any visit limit, and the expected copay.

Record the result in the intake system as verified, not covered, or needs follow-up. If the visit is not covered, offer the self-pay price list before the appointment.

Never type a full member ID into chat, email or an AI assistant. Use the secure intake form instead.
