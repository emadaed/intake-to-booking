---
id: POL-002
title: Cancellations and no-shows
status: current
effective_date: 2026-09-01
owner: Operations
---
A cancellation made with at least 24 hours' notice, meaning at least 24 hours before the appointment, has no fee.

A cancellation made less than 24 hours before the appointment, or a no-show, is charged a $50 late fee. The first late cancellation for any patient is waived.

After two no-shows in a row, a manager must review the case before another appointment is booked.

Staff can waive a fee only when the problem was on the clinic side, such as a video platform failure, and must note the reason in the patient record.
