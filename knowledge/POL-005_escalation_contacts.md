title: Who to contact and when to escalate
status: current
effective_date: 2026-06-01
owner: Operations
---
Billing and payment questions go to the billing lead. Questions about clinical care go to the clinical supervisor. Platform and scheduling system problems go to operations.

If a patient may be in immediate danger, call local emergency services first, then tell the clinical supervisor as soon as possible.

If you are not sure who owns a question, ask the operations manager instead of guessing.
