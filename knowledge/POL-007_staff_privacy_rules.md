---
id: POL-007
title: Staff privacy rules for chat tools and AI assistants
status: current
effective_date: 2026-06-01
owner: Privacy officer
---
Do not paste patient names, dates of birth, member IDs, phone numbers, email addresses, or health details into chat tools, shared documents, or AI assistants.

Refer to a case by its internal case number instead.

If patient information is shared in the wrong place by mistake, tell the privacy officer the same day. Do not try to fix it quietly.
