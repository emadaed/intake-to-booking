title: New patient intake checklist
status: current
effective_date: 2026-06-01
owner: Intake team
---
For each new patient, collect the preferred name, the state of residence, the preferred appointment times, and the insurance details through the secure intake form.

The state of residence matters because providers are licensed by state. Confirm that a licensed provider is available for that state before booking.

By phone, record only the general reason for the visit. Do not collect detailed health history; the provider does that during the visit.
