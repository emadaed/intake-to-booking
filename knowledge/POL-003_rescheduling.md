id: POL-003
title: Rescheduling appointments
status: current
effective_date: 2026-06-01
owner: Operations
---
A patient can reschedule up to two times per appointment at no charge, if the request is made at least 24 hours ahead.

Reschedule in the scheduling system, not by editing the old booking. Keep the same provider when possible.

The system sends reminders 48 hours and 24 hours before each appointment. After a reschedule, check that the new reminders were created.
